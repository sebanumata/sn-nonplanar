Privacy policy · sn_non-planar

Last updated: November 13, 2026

This page explains what I do with your email when you join the sn_non-planar waitlist. It's short because I keep very little.

Who is responsible for your data

Sebastian Numata (sebanumata), based in Posadas, Misiones, Argentina.
Contact: sebanumata@gmail.com

What I keep

  • Your email address.
  • The date you signed up and the page you signed up from.
  • The language of the page you used, so I can write to you in it.
  • Whether you confirmed your email and when, along with the code in the confirmation link.

I don't ask for your name, phone number or anything else. Giving me your email is voluntary: if you don't, I simply can't tell you about the launch.

What I use it for

  • Sending you a single email to confirm that the address is yours. If you don't confirm it, I won't write to you again.
  • Letting you know when sn_non-planar launches and sending you the special price for the list.
  • Sharing development news from time to time.

I don't sell, rent or hand over your email to anyone. I don't use it for anything outside this list.

Where it is stored

The list lives in a Google Sheets spreadsheet in my Google account. Google stores that data on its servers, which may be outside Argentina (for example, in the United States). By signing up, you expressly agree to your email being stored there.

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, United States). Like any web server, GitHub may log technical data about your visit, such as your IP address, for security. I don't use cookies or tracking tools on this page.

How long I keep it

For as long as the list is active, or until you ask me to delete you. If I stop using the list, I delete it.

Your rights

At any time and free of charge, you can:

  • Unsubscribe: every email I send explains how. Replying "UNSUBSCRIBE" is also enough.
  • Find out what data I have about you (right of access).
  • Correct it or ask me to delete it (rectification and erasure).

Write to me at sebanumata@gmail.com. I answer access requests within 10 calendar days, and corrections or deletions within 5 business days, as set by Argentina's Personal Data Protection Law (Law 25,326).

The right of access is free and can be exercised every six months, or sooner if you have a legitimate interest (Law 25,326, art. 14, sec. 3).

Argentina's Agency for Access to Public Information (AAIP), the authority that enforces Law 25,326, handles complaints from anyone who believes their personal data rights were not respected. If you live in the European Union or the United Kingdom, you also have the rights granted by the GDPR, including filing a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Changes

If I change anything in this policy, I update the date at the top. If the change is important, I'll let you know by email.
